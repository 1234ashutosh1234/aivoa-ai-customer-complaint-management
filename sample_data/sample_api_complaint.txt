DEFECT AND OUT-OF-SPECIFICATION (OOS) SUMMARY:
During the mandatory sampling and incoming quality verification of drum shipments under SOP-RM-104, our QC sampling technicians discovered physical particulate anomalies:
1. Foreign Particulate Matter: Upon opening polyethylene inner drum liner #14, visible black speck contamination was detected on the surface and embedded within the top 5 cm of the white crystalline powder. Fourier Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FTIR) microscopy performed on isolated specks tentatively matches fluoropolymer / Viton gasket degradation material.
2. Particle Size Distribution (PSD) Deviation: Sieve analysis for Drum #18 revealed d90 = 64 µm against our agreed finished drug formulation raw material specification of d90 <= 45 µm. This severe coarseness prevents homogeneous direct compression in our high-speed rotary tablet presses.

REGULATORY & COMPLIANCE IMPACT:
This consignment represents an unformulated Active Pharmaceutical Ingredient. All 50 drums have been placed on strict Incoming Quality Control (IQC) Quarantine Hold (Reject Tag #QR-9921) in Warehouse Bay C. No finished drug product batches have been formulated using this lot.

ACTION REQUIRED FROM API MANUFACTURER:
1. Initiate formal Root Cause Analysis (RCA) regarding process filtration, centrifuge seals, and micronizer gasket integrity.
2. Provide disposition instructions for the return or destruction of all 50 drums.
3. Supply full Batch Production & Control Record (BPCR) review for LOT-PARA-2026-0814.
